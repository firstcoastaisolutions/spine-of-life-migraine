HIPAA Compliance Statement

At Spine of Life Upper Cervical Chiropractic, under the clinical leadership of Dr. Kevin Pecca, D.C., protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of your Protected Health Information (PHI) is a fundamental duty. This statement outlines our stringent compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act, and applicable Florida state health privacy statutes.

enhanced_encryption Safeguards & Digital Protections

Encrypted Imaging & CBCT Data

All Blair Upper Cervical spinographic evaluations and cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) 3D volumetric images are archived within dedicated AES-256 encrypted DICOM storage environments with multi-layered perimeter access.

HIPAA-Compliant EHR Infrastructure

Our digital charting, billing records, and schedule workflows operate via Jane App—a certified electronic health records system featuring SOC-2 Type II verification, zero unauthorized third-party tracking, and continuous data backup.

Staff Access & Minimum Necessary Standard

Every clinic employee, medical scribe, and administrative associate undergoes rigorous annual HIPAA data security training. Internal access to health information is strictly role-delimited in adherence to the federal "Minimum Necessary" threshold.

Your Rights Under Federal HIPAA Law

  • check_circle Right to Inspect and Copy: You possess the absolute legal right to inspect and obtain copies of your electronic health files, diagnostic X-rays, thermographic scans, and billing summaries.
  • check_circle Right to Request Amendments: You may petition an amendment if you believe diagnostic or clerical notations in your patient record are incomplete or inaccurate.
  • check_circle Right to an Accounting of Disclosures: You may request a detailed ledger of all disclosures made of your PHI outside standard treatment, payment, and healthcare operations.
  • check_circle Right to Confidential Communications: You may elect how and where we contact you (e.g., cell phone only, no voicemail messages, or specific postal routing).

Section 3

Terms of Care & Clinic Policies

The clinical objective of Spine of Life Upper Cervical Chiropractic is the precise detection, neurological analysis, and gentle biomechanical correction of upper cervical subluxations utilizing the Blair Upper Cervical technique. By initiating care with our practice, you acknowledge and agree to the following terms:

1. Consultation vs. Ongoing Treatment Scope

An initial consultation and preliminary neuro-articular examination constitute an evaluative assessment. A mutual doctor-patient therapeutic relationship is formally instituted only once comprehensive diagnostics have been conducted, contraindications evaluated, and informed written consent executed.

2. Blair Upper Cervical Protocol Specifics

Blair Upper Cervical Chiropractic is characterized by high-precision, low-force biomechanical adjustments of the C1 (Atlas) and C2 (Axis) vertebrae. There is zero forceful twisting, cracking, or aggressive spinal manipulation. While this approach is remarkably gentle, transient physiological responses—such as temporary muscle tenderness or fatigue—may occur as nervous system equilibrium is reestablished.

3. Appointment Scheduling & Cancellation Notice

Because Dr. Kevin Pecca, D.C. dedicates focused, individual appointment blocks for each patient examination and post-adjustment resting period, we respectfully require a minimum of 24 business hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. This courtesy enables waiting patients with acute neurological symptoms to receive care.

4. Payment Policies & Direct-Pay / Superbill Model

Spine of Life Upper Cervical Chiropractic operates as an independent private practice committed to clinical autonomy and uncompromised care standards. Payment is due at the time service is rendered. Upon request, our office gladly provides itemized statements (superbills) complete with standard ICD-10 diagnostic codes and CPT procedural notations that patients may submit directly to their private insurers for potential out-of-network reimbursement.

Acute Medical Emergencies
