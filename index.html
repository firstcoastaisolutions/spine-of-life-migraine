Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Blair Upper Cervical care before your initial visit.

What is Blair Upper Cervical? Is there any popping or cracking? expand_more

No, absolutely not. Blair Upper Cervical care does not involve cracking, twisting, or popping the neck. Instead, it utilizes specialized 3D imaging to pinpoint the exact millimeters and degrees of your C1 Atlas vertebra misalignment. Dr. Pecca delivers a gentle, feather-light touch measured in ounces of pressure, allowing the vertebra to reset into its natural anatomical position without sudden manual torque.

How long does an initial consultation take? expand_more

Your free initial consultation takes approximately 15 to 30 minutes. Dr. Pecca thoroughly reviews your health history, prior MRI reports, concussion timeline, and symptom severity. If you are a candidate for upper cervical care, advanced 3D CBCT scans and neurological thermography will be performed in our St. Augustine clinic to map your precise cervical anatomy.

Will insurance cover my care? expand_more

We provide all necessary itemized superbills and diagnostic documentation for you to submit to your major medical insurance provider, HSA, or FSA for direct reimbursement. Because our ultra-precise 3D CBCT imaging and customized Blair adjustments exceed standard HMO limitations, all transparent pricing is reviewed before any services begin.

Why haven't regular neurologists or doctors told me about the Atlas? expand_more

Standard medical school training focuses heavily on pharmaceutical intervention for neurological conditions. Additionally, conventional supine MRIs and generic cervical X-rays are read for structural fractures or tumors—frequently missing rotational 2-to-3 millimeter subluxations of the Atlas that pinch the brainstem and restrict venous CSF drainage. Blair Upper Cervical is an advanced postgraduate field calibrated to diagnose and correct these mechanical misalignments.
